Taipei, Taiwan 台灣 · 台北

I am an AI engineer specializing in Large Language Model (LLM) post-training and fine-tuning, with hands-on experience across SFT, DPO/SimPO, RLHF/PPO, and parameter-efficient methods such as LoRA and QLoRA. My work centers on adapting and aligning LLMs for downstream tasks and on building agent systems on top of fine-tuned models. I bring a broader background in NLP, Transformer architectures, Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), and traditional Machine Learning, which informs my approach to model design and reasoning.

I believe mature techniques such as classical ML and GNNs remain highly relevant in the LLM era, offering complementary strengths in structured knowledge modeling, interpretability, and controllability. I actively explore frontier directions in LLM training, with particular interest in the joint training of Knowledge Graphs and LLMs as a path toward stronger structured reasoning and factual grounding. By combining these approaches with modern post-training methods, I aim to build next-generation AI systems that are more controllable, reasoning-aware, and better aligned with real-world applications.

我是一名 AI 工程師，專注在大型語言模型（LLM）的 post-training 與微調， 實務經驗涵蓋 SFT、DPO/SimPO、RLHF/PPO，以及 LoRA、QLoRA 這類 parameter-efficient 方法。 工作重心放在把 LLM 對應到下游任務做適配與對齊，並在微調後的模型上構建 agent 系統。 NLP、Transformer 架構、Graph Neural Networks（GNNs）、以及傳統機器學習也是我的底子， 這些背景決定了我看待模型設計與推理問題的方式。

我認為傳統 ML 與 GNN 這類成熟技術在 LLM 時代仍然非常相關 —— 它們在結構化知識建模、可解釋性與可控性上提供 LLM 缺乏的互補能力。 我在 LLM 訓練的前沿方向上持續探索，特別關注知識圖譜與 LLM 聯合訓練 這條路徑，把它當成往更強的結構化推理與事實對齊邁進的方向。 目標是把這些方法跟現代 post-training 結合， 做出更可控、更具推理意識、更貼近真實應用的下一代 AI 系統。

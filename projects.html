Domain Knowledge Internalization via KG × LLM Joint Training — Insurance Advisor as a Case Study

領域知識內化 —— KG × LLM 聯合訓練 · 以保險顧問為案例

A research exploration of an alternative to RAG for structured domains: instead of retrieving knowledge at inference time, jointly train a knowledge graph with a small LLM (Qwen3-0.6B) so that structured product knowledge lives inside the model weights. The assistant answers without any retrieval step at inference.

Insurance is the case study — a domain where retrieval-based answers tend to be "literally relevant but structurally misaligned". The full training pipeline (CLM → SFT → DPO → PPO) goes deep on a single product (Taiwan reimbursement-based medical insurance) rather than spreading wide.

一個針對強結構領域的 RAG 替代方案研究：不在 inference 時做檢索， 而是把知識圖譜跟小型 LLM（Qwen3-0.6B）聯合訓練， 讓結構化的商品知識直接住在模型權重裡 —— 推論時不再有 retrieval 環節。

以保險為案例 —— 這是一個 retrieval-based 答案常常「字面相關但結構錯位」的領域。 完整訓練線（CLM → SFT → DPO → PPO）針對單一商品（台灣實支實付醫療險）做深度， 而不是橫向覆蓋。

Sales Conversation Trainer — Multi-step Reasoning + Multi-Dimensional Scoring

Sales Conversation Trainer —— 多步推理 + 多維度評分

A sales training system: the user plays the sales rep while an AI plays a realistic customer; after each session the system scores the rep's performance and returns actionable feedback. Simulation is the means — the goal is sharpening real-conversation skills. The core design uses multi-step reasoning to decompose the customer's response into separate dimensions (persona, mood, motivation, conversation flow) so the simulated customer behaves like a specific person rather than an averaged-out polite assistant.

Major players in the financial industry have expressed strong interest in adopting the system.

一個業務訓練系統：使用者扮演業務，AI 扮演擬真的顧客； 每場對話結束後，系統會對業務的表現評分並回饋具體可改進的方向。 模擬只是手段，磨練業務的真實對話能力才是目的。 核心設計用多步推理把顧客的回應拆成不同維度 （persona、情緒、動機、對話流程）， 讓模擬出來的顧客像「某個具體的人」，而不是一個被平均化的禮貌助理。

金融產業多個主要業者已對採用本系統表達高度興趣。

Tenfold AI — Legal Advisor Chatbot & Multi-step Architecture

Tenfold AI —— 法律顧問 Chatbot 與多步驟架構

The founding product I built at Tenfold AI: a legal advisor chatbot for a domain where answers must cite the right statute and address the actual question. A general-purpose RAG pipeline isn't enough — the retrieval layer needs to be tuned for legal context, and the answer needs to come with evidence the user can trust.

I designed a multi-step architecture with rerank, regulation check, and other verification tools, capped by a verified confidence score attached to each response — so users not only get an answer, but understand how grounded that answer is in the underlying law.

我在 Tenfold AI 打造的最一開始產品：一款法律顧問 Chatbot —— 法律是一個答案必須引到正確條文、必須對應到使用者實際問題的領域。 通用 RAG pipeline 在這場景不夠用 —— retrieval 要為法律情境調過，回答還要附上使用者信得過的依據。

我設計了一條多步驟架構，搭配 rerank、法規檢查等驗證工具， 每個回答還會附上經過驗證的信心程度 —— 讓使用者拿到的不只是答案，還包含這個答案在底層法條上站得有多穩。

G3-RAG — Multi-step Reasoning RAG over Knowledge Graphs

G3-RAG —— 基於知識圖譜的多步推理 RAG

A graph-guided Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework that addresses information loss in long-context LLMs by constructing a structured knowledge graph via relation extraction and combining it with graph-based retrieval — improving multi-hop reasoning, multi-source integration, and answer accuracy.

Received faculty approval and proceeded to journal submission.

一個以知識圖譜為導向的 Retrieval-Augmented Generation 框架， 針對長文本下 LLM 的資訊遺失問題 —— 透過關係抽取建構結構化知識圖譜， 結合圖檢索方法，提升多跳推理、多源知識整合與回答準確度。

通過口試，期刊投稿中。
